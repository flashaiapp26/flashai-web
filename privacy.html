Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how FlashAI ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using FlashAI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Content you create: Flashcard decks, notes, and PDF documents you upload to generate flashcards.
  • Usage data: Study sessions, streaks, card review history, and preferences (e.g. notification time).
  • Device information: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers for push notifications.

We may also collect information automatically when you use the app, such as crash reports and performance data, to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the FlashAI service.
  • Generate AI-powered flashcards from your uploaded content using third-party AI providers.
  • Send you daily study reminders and important account notifications.
  • Process payments and manage your subscription via RevenueCat.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Respond to your support requests and communications.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

FlashAI uses artificial intelligence to generate flashcards from your notes and PDFs. Your content is sent to AI providers (such as Anthropic Claude) to process your request. We do not use your content to train AI models.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database and authentication (data stored in the EU).
  • Google (OAuth) — Optional sign-in with Google.
  • RevenueCat — Subscription and payment management.
  • Expo / Apple / Google — Push notification delivery.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers located in the European Union. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) so users can only access their own data.
  • Secure authentication tokens with automatic expiry.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data — including your profile, decks, cards, and study history — is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at flashai.app26@gmail.com or use our account deletion form.

7. Children's Privacy

FlashAI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, via in-app notification. Continued use of FlashAI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
